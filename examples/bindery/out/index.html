How the same structure adapts

Navigation by screen size

Four breakpoints chosen around the devices booksellers actually use: a phone at the shelves, an iPad on the counter, a laptop in the back office. Nothing is the desktop shrunk.

Phone · under 768

Bottom tab bar, five labelled tabs, reachable with a thumb. Page title and one primary action in the header.

Tablet · 768 to 1199

An 88 px rail, icon above label. Leaves the width for content on an iPad in portrait at the till.

Laptop 1200 to 1599 · Desktop 1600 and up

A 248 px sidebar with labels; its foot shows the shop and who is signed in. Detail opens in a right panel (dashed). On desktop the panel stays open and content is capped at 1280 px.

Bindery · Step 2 of 3 · Visual system

Bindery visual system

Warm paper, ruled lists instead of cards, one indigo for actions, and every order drawn as a trail.

Every text colour passes WCAG AA on white (checked by the generator). Status is always carried by words and shape as well as colour.
