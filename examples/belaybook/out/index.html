Cross-links

A booking opens its class. A class shows your other bookings that day. The Home headline goes straight to the next booking and its check-in code.

Outside the sections

Sign in. Public timetable for people who are not members yet. Not found. Payment errors that say what to try next.

The API needs two additions

1. Spaces left per class in the timetable list, not only on the class. 2. A booking endpoint that returns the check-in code.

How the same structure adapts

Navigation by screen size

Members book on their phones, often in the changing room, so the phone gets the most care. The front desk runs a tablet; staff schedule classes on a laptop.

Phone · under 768

Bottom tab bar, five labelled tabs. The next booking and its code are never more than one tap away.

Tablet · 768 to 1199

An 88 px rail. At the front desk it shows today's classes with names, in landscape.

Laptop 1200 to 1599 · Desktop 1600 and up

A 248 px sidebar. Class detail opens in a right panel beside the week's timetable.

Belaybook · Step 2 of 3 · Visual system

Belaybook visual system

Chalk white, warm greys, one rust colour for actions, and the week drawn as a strip.

Every text colour passes WCAG AA on white (checked by the generator). Full, booked and spaces left are always words, never colour alone.
